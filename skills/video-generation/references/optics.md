# Optics

Two levers: shot size and focal length. Never put millimetres in the prompt — use FOV degrees from the anchor table.

## Shot sizes

| Abbr | In frame |
|---|---|
| ECU | one detail: eyes, clasp, link, hand |
| CU | full face or one element large |
| MCU | head and shoulders |
| MS | roughly to the waist |
| WS | full figure + surroundings |
| EWS | scale, location |

## Focal-length intent (reasoning only)

| Look | Effect | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Wide (24–35mm) | space, slight stretch | action, establish |
| Normal (50mm) | natural | realism, coverage |
| Portrait (85mm) | soft bokeh, separation | emotion |
| Tele (135mm+) | compression | observation, sport |

## FOV anchor table

Use only these steps in prompts (not 23° — pick 18 or 29).

| FOV | mm equiv | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 180 | fisheye | POV, dream |
| 107 | 14–16mm | large interiors, epic establish |
| 84 | 20–24mm | establish, group |
| 63 | 28–35mm | reportage, wide observation |
| 47 | 40–50mm | neutral medium |
| 29 | 75–85mm | portrait compression, dialogue bust |
| 18 | 100–135mm | close portrait, identity |
| 12 | 180–200mm | hands, product macro |
| 8 | 300–400mm | observation, broadcast |

Multishot: FOV per segment + "no drift mid-segment". CAMERA block sits third among core layers.

## Optical recipes

**Hidden-camera / observation** — all three at once or it reads as a normal long lens: foreground occlusion 20–30% out of focus; haze between camera and subject; 8–12° vantage far away. Change occlusion type between beats; keep one vantage.

**Sports broadcast** — 8°, handheld 1–2cm tremor, "anchored at distance, finding the action".

**Snake cam (detail-on-wide)** — 84° low angle against a small object; foreground exaggerates, background recedes.

**Intimate wide** — 63–84° on a close face; surroundings readable.

**Tele air column** — at 8–12°: dust or heat shimmer in the compressed space between camera and subject.

**Product macro** — 12° ECU, 20–30cm out; focus on specular light travel across the surface.

## Camera, light, colour

- White balance in Kelvin, fixed in scene: 3200K tungsten · 4000K mixed · 5600K daylight · 8500K cool shade
- No equipment names — describe the look
- Colour as material + light beam + role
- Background in layers: foreground / midground / background
- Prefer camera on the shadow side; state operator axis
