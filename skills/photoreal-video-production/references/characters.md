# Photoreal character sheets

Use `video-production/references/sheets/character-sheet.md` as written: it is photoreal by default (natural no-makeup skin with pores, `#504f50` studio backdrop, neutral studio light, studio-photograph wording). What to add for a photoreal video:

- **Casting.** Before the sheet, check the person's age and look against what the scenes ask of them (a couple in a romantic beat reads wrong if they look too old for it). Put the age in the sheet and in every still.
- **Real people only as references.** A supplied photo is the identity source; never upload real photos of minors.
