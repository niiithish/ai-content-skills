# Script Generation

Write short-form ads people finish. Default output is a **spoken UGC script** — a friend on camera, not a brand film.

A concept essay with no spoken lines is a failed script. A spec sheet read aloud is a failed script.

## Non-negotiables

1. **Spoken first.** The idea *is* the script. Even if they say "just the idea," give the spoken draft.
2. **Hook under 8 words.** Complete thought. Opens a loop the script closes. Write three from [references/hooks.md](references/hooks.md), pick one, do not rewrite it.
3. **One problem, 2–3 proofs.** Not a feature list.
4. **Zero ad voice.** No "introducing", "game-changing", "obsessed", or three-adjective stacks. If a person would not say it to a friend, rewrite.
5. **No ambiguous pronouns** when contrasting this product with a worse one. See anti-confusion below.

## Workflow

1. Product truth from the page/URL: differentiator, dose, proof, what failed before. Skip contradictory claims rather than picking a side.
2. Awareness stage (table below) — decides the open.
3. Three hooks, different mechanics → pick one. Patterns: [references/hooks.md](references/hooks.md).
4. Map the arc, then draft 21–45 seconds spoken (~50–120 words).
5. Retention pass (section below).
6. Audit with [references/audit.md](references/audit.md) before showing the user.
7. Cut list + [references/tracker.md](references/tracker.md) only if they asked for AI video, animation, or a storyboard.

Missing brand voice, audience, or KPI: note the assumption in one line and write anyway.

## Awareness stage

| Stage | Viewer already knows | Script opens on |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | no problem felt | relatable moment that names the problem |
| Problem-aware | pain, no solutions | agitate briefly, reveal category |
| Solution-aware | category, been burned | differentiate vs what they tried |
| Product-aware | this brand, not bought | proof, objections, risk reversal |
| Most aware | ready | offer + curiosity, not fake urgency |

**Solution-aware is most often misdiagnosed.** Owners of cheap versions do not need the problem explained — differentiate against past failures.

## Emotional arc

```
Hook     attention / recognition
Problem  frustration (specific, not melodramatic)
Solution hope — product enters casually
Proof    trust — 2–3 concrete details
CTA      invitation, not a hard sell
```

## What good looks like

**Hook:** "I'll be honest, I quit fish oil because of the burps."

**Script (~40s):**

I'll be honest, I quit fish oil because of the burps.

I'd take some cheap capsule with breakfast, sit at my desk, and by lunch I'm tasting fish. In a meeting. Not ideal.

Then I switched to Tata 1mg Salmon Omega 3. It's 1000mg, triglyceride form, 180 EPA, 120 DHA. One capsule a day.

And the no fish burps thing? Yeah. It's real. Easy to take. No odour, no fish smell. 4.4 from over a thousand ratings, so it's not just me.

If you bounced off fish oil last time, try this one. Your 3pm self will thank you.

Why it works: hook unchanged; problem is a scene not a slogan; product walks in as "then I switched"; proofs are physical (form, burps, rating); CTA is an invitation.

## Retention (TikTok)

Script for **completion**, then shares. Likes do not matter.

| Window | Risk | Defense |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | scroll | first frame already moving; hook is a complete thought |
| 8–10s | "so what" | pattern interrupt — new angle, prop, or a line that resets attention |
| Mid | tension resolved too early | keep one question open until proof |
| Last 3s | skip before CTA | product name one beat earlier than feels natural |

- Sound-off still has to work: 3–5 text overlays that carry the story alone.
- End soft: "try this one" / "I'll drop it in my bio" / "tell me if this happened to you."
- A natural comment or stitch prompt is useful. "Shop now" and "only 3 left" are not.

## Voice

Every line must pass: *would a real person say this out loud, on their phone, while making coffee?*

Native: "honestly", "okay so", a specific Tuesday, visible skepticism before the payoff.  
Ad-like: brand-first, legal diction, "I can't believe this worked", studio-perfect sentences.

## Anti-confusion rule

Viewer hears audio once. Any pronoun that could point at the product will be heard as the product.

Broken: *"I used to buy these constantly… Then your neck turns green."* → product sounds defective.

Fixed: *"Every gold chain I owned before this one died in about two weeks. Green neck, patchy plating, straight in the bin."*

Use all three: explicit noun phrase · temporal boundary ("before this one") · terminal past verdict. On screen, the old thing must look physically different from the hero.

## Numbers and claims

Concrete beats vague ("four months" > "months and months"). Stay inside what the user can defend. Flag every claim. Never invent reviews, ratings, or certifications.

## Output

Always, in this order:

1. **Spoken script** — words only, line breaks = breaths. No stage directions in the script body.
2. **Tone and delivery** — who is talking, pace, what not to punch.
3. **Visual pairing** — 4–6 bullets: first frame, problem beat, product enter, proof, CTA. Include overlay lines.
4. **Alt approaches** — two other hooks from different mechanics, one line each on when to use them.
5. **Caption** — under 150 characters, 3–5 niche hashtags, conversational. No hashtag stuffing.
6. **Claims checklist** — what to verify before publish.

If they asked for variations: three full scripts, each a different hook mechanic. Otherwise one lead script + two alt hooks.

If they asked for AI video / animation / storyboard, append:

7. **Cut list** — table: # · duration · line · shot · environment. 2–3s per cut, new environment or subject at least every other cut. Nobody needs to speak on camera (lip-sync is the AI tell). Hand the cut list to **video-generation**.

## Revision map

| Complaint | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| boring / people will sleep | no scene, no interrupt | specific moment + cut at 8–10s |
| reads like an ad | marketing register | rewrite as speech, one problem |
| confusing | ambiguous referent | anti-confusion rule |
| too long | feature dump | one problem, 2–3 proofs, cut words |
| not my style | wrong register | ask for one reference ad |
| concept, no script | withheld the draft | write the spoken script |

Rewrite from the arc. Do not patch individual lines.
