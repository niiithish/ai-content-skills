# Hooks

First 1–3 seconds. Write three from **different** mechanics, pick one, do not rewrite it.

A hook is a complete thought in **under 8 words spoken**. If those eight words do not create a reason to stay, it is a setup, not a hook.

## Mechanics

| Mechanic | Shape | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Story / confession | "I'll be honest, I quit…" | Strongest default for burned buyers |
| Relatable pain | A specific scene, not "if you struggle with" | Self-recognition |
| Curiosity gap | Unanswered question or "I was today years old…" | Loop must close in the script |
| Contrarian | "Everyone says X. Here's why that's wrong." | Payoff in the next line |
| Objection-first | "I did not believe the no-burp thing either." | Names disbelief first |
| Failure confession | "I have thrown out four of these." | Needs anti-confusion rule |
| Borrowed question | "Someone asked me if…" | Good for sceptical products |
| Duration flex | "Four months. Never taken it off." | Concrete; implies the objection |
| Mid-action | Visual already moving; first line is mid-thought | When the first frame is the hook |
| Bold claim | Specific and checkable | Not "this changed my life" |
| Number-based | One number in the hook | Avoid "3 reasons why" list ads |

## Contrast

If the lead is confession or pain, the two alts should be curiosity and contrarian (or objection-first). Do not offer three rewordings of the same line.

## Testing

Each hook must:

1. Create unresolved tension
2. Stay under 8 words spoken
3. Name a specific person and a specific problem
4. Survive a flat read with no music
5. Fit only this product
6. Open a loop the script actually closes

Broken promise kills retention at the pivot, not the hook — fix the pairing.

## Anti-patterns

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| "Are you tired of…" | instantly an ad |
| "Introducing the…" | brand-first |
| "I'm obsessed with…" | empty enthusiasm |
| "This changed my life" | unfalsifiable |
| "I can't believe this worked" | paid-ad tell |
| "POV: you…" | worn template |
| "3 reasons why…" | blog post, not a conversation |
| Question answerable with "no" | permission to scroll |
| Brand or product name in the hook | they came for a person, not a logo |
