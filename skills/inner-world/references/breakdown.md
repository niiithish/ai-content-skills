## 2. The Inner-World Look: Pasteable Style Guide

### Master Style Block (Copy-Paste into Prompts)
> **Style & Render Guide:** High-end 3D macro CGI inside a stylized biological world, rendered with Octane/Redshift-style physically based subsurface scattering (SSS), refractive glass/amber shaders, and soft volumetric backlighting. It is a clean, tactile, fantastical toy-etic stylized anatomy—never gory, never wet-visceral horror, and never a flat 2D medical diagram. Every tissue wall, bubble, and vessel looks like warm translucent silicone, pearlescent blown glass, soft peach wax, or liquid honey lit from behind by a warm golden-white key light. Shallow macro depth of field (`f/2.0`) with creamy foreground and background bokeh, floating golden micro-bubbles, and crisp specular rim highlights.

---

### Detailed Visual Breakdown

#### 1. Render Style
- **Relation to the exterior Pixar-style world:** Uses the exact same soft, rounded, high-subsurface-scattering 3D shading philosophy as the character scenes, translated to a macro endoscopic scale. There are no sharp polygons, no clinical flat textures, and no gross-out biological realism (no veins, no blood clots, no slimy mucus strings).
- **Level of abstraction:** A **stylized luxury-cosmetic fantasy of anatomy** (similar to high-end skincare or supplement CGI commercials). Cells, biofilm walls, and parasites are simplified into tactile, sculptural forms—pearl-like bubbles, gummy-candy-like spiky blobs, blown-glass tubes, and jewel-like amber capsules.

#### 2. Materials (Shader-by-Shader)
- **Biofilm Walls, Domes & Bubbles:**
  - *Translucency & SSS:* High subsurface scattering (`80%` milky opacity) like white pearl glass, steamed milk foam, or soft white silicone. When backlit, light diffuses through the walls in a warm peach-orange glow (`#F7B286`).
  - *Surface & Gloss:* Smooth, taut, high-gloss clear-coat finish (`roughness ~0.12`) with crisp studio-like white specular reflections. In cross-section (Clip 1) or trabecular tunnels (Clip 3), the interior wall structure is spongy and porous like aerated white chocolate or smooth bone trabeculae.
  - * Dome Variant (Clip 3):* The "protective layer" bell-jar dome is clearer (`~40%` opacity), moulded with concentric horizontal ribs like frosted borosilicate glass.
- **Tunnel Walls (Healthy / Neutral):**
  - *Material:* Concentric, spiraling folds of translucent peach-cream tissue (`#E8B89A` to `#FDECE2`), resembling silk ribbons or smooth layered wax lit from the distant center of the tunnel.
- **Tubes / Capillaries (Clip 1):**
  - *Material:* Clear, slightly peach-tinted blown glass or flexible medical-grade silicone tubing with fine longitudinal striations along the inner wall, strong index of refraction (`IOR ~1.5`), and bright white-gold edge reflections.
- **Golden Nutrient / Oil Spheres:**
  - *Material:* Refractive liquid amber-gold spheres (`#F59E0B` to `#FDE047`, `IOR ~1.48`) with tiny trapped effervescent micro-bubbles inside and a hot white-yellow core glow.
- **Parasites & Inflamed Nodes ("Sick" Elements):**
  - *Cutaway & Tunnel Parasites (Clip 1 & 3):* Matte-velvet to semi-glossy gummy blobs (`#6D1A29` to `#8C2D3E`) shaped like plump oval microbes with 6–10 stubby, rounded pseudopod spikes or raspberry-like bumps. Heavy reddish-pink subsurface scattering on the edges so they look rubbery rather than slimy.
  - *Swollen Red Node (Clip 1, Shot 1.6):* Pitted, spongy crimson-pink flesh (`#9E2A3A`) with a velvety micro-bumpy displacement map and a wet, darker crimson central crater.
  - *Dark Biofilm Sludge / Inflammation Wall (Clip 2):* Coagulated, glossy tar-maroon/black-cherry crust (`#2A0710` to `#4A0D1C`) with wet specular highlights and embedded golden-amber grit.
- **Softgel "Ships" (Clip 3):**
  - *Hull:* Elongated torpedo/bullet-shaped translucent ruby-amber gelatin capsule (`#8F1907` hull grading to `#D9530F`), smooth and glass-glossy (`roughness ~0.05`).
  - *Inner Detail:* A blazing golden-orange energy core (`#FBBF24`) surrounded by concentric glowing gold wireframe rings and swirling golden dust particles visible through the refractive shell.
  - *Mechanical Trim:* Thin, delicate rose-gold / bronze metallic wire rails hugging the fuselage and two rear/side cylindrical thrusters emitting soft pink-white/orange plasma cones.
- **Amber Oil Volume & Sea (Clip 3):**
  - *Material:* Rich, viscous liquid honey / dark amber oil (`#B45309` to `#F59E0B`) with intense golden subsurface scattering, small suspended spherical droplets, and gentle, syrupy surface ripples.

- **Base / Neutral Tissue Palette:**

- **Palette Shift Across Beats:** "Sick" shots contrast pale peach-cream walls against dark, light-absorbing maroon-crimson and black-cherry masses (`#2B0710`–`#7A2234`). As soon as the "healing" beat starts, dark maroon is overwhelmed by high-luminance, self-illuminated amber, gold, and warm white (`#D97706`–`#FFFBEB`), shifting the overall frame exposure up by +1.5 stops.

#### 4. Light
- **Primary Key / Backlight:** Almost every inner-world shot is **backlit from the center-depth of the frame** (the vanishing point at the end of the tunnel or the sky behind the biofilm horizon). This drives strong warm-peach and golden subsurface glow through the translucent walls and spheres.
- **Rim Light:** Strong, clean warm-white and golden rim lights (`#FFE8C8`) trace the contours of every bubble, tube, parasite, and softgel.
- **Internal Bioluminescence & Bloom:** Golden spheres, the interior of thesoftgel ship, and particle explosions emit their own warm amber-gold light with a soft, creamy optical bloom/glow radius (~10–15% of object width). Overall frame brightness is high-key and warm (even in dark tunnel shots, the central core or golden spray provides a high-contrast radiant focal point).

#### 5. Depth and Focus
- **Depth of Field:** Extremely shallow macro depth of field (`f/1.8–f/2.4` equivalent). Objects within 10% of the camera lens (foreground bubbles, passing parasites, foreground tubes) blur into soft, creamy circular bokeh; background structures dissolve into smooth peach-and-cream atmospheric haze.
- **Floating Particulates:** Every shot contains 30–100 tiny out-of-focus floating golden dust motes, micro-bubbles, or sparkling amber droplets drifting through the volume to sell the underwater/microscopic scale.
- **Scale Cues:** The viewer feels ~1–5 millimeters tall—small enough that a single softgel capsule looks like a sleek spacecraft and capillaries look like highway overpasses.

#### 6. Motion Style
- **Camera Motion:** Smooth, weightless, continuous spline paths—either **hurtling forward down a central cylindrical tunnel with a gentle barrel roll** (15°–45° spiral), **pulling backward along a tubular network**, or **tracking in a tight third-person video-game chase cam** behind the softgel ship. Transitions rely on sharp speed-ramps (slow drift accelerating into a sudden 0.3 s lunge into a target).
- **Object Motion:** Viscous, damped, low-gravity fluid dynamics—parasites bob and squirm slowly as if suspended in warm gel; oil droplets rise with smooth buoyancy; biofilm domes shatter into thousands of crisp, radially outward-shooting golden spark particles that decelerate in the fluid.

#### 7. HUD and Labels
- **Typeface:** Clean, geometric, all-caps sans-serif (similar to *Eurostile Extended*, *Bank Gothic*, or *Montserrat Bold/Medium*) with wide letter-spacing (`+0.08em` to `+0.15em`). In `BLACK SEED OIL` (Shot 3.10), the letters use a double-line/inline engraved metallic-gold outline style; all other HUD labels are crisp flat white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Graphic Framing:**
  - Status words (`LOCKED`, `CLEEAR`, `PROTECTIVE LAYER`, `CARVACOL`) sit inside thin `1.5px` white pill-shaped rounded rectangles (`border-radius: 999px`).
  - Circular targeting reticles feature thin concentric white rings (`1px`–`2px` stroke), dashed degree ticks, thicker 90° arc segments, and a small circular badge above the text containing either an exclamation mark `!` or a checkmark `✓`.
  - Callout lines use thin `1px` white horizontal rules terminated by small solid white circles (`•`), or a 90° leader line pointing directly to the softgel nose (`OREGANO OIL / 85% / CARVACOL`).
- **Placement & Animation:** Placed dead-center in the 9:16 safe zone (between 35% and 65% vertical height) so they never clash with bottom captions. Elements animate in via quick 0.25 s masked vertical slide-ups, radial ring draws, and rapid numeric counter roll-ups (`65` → `85`), and scale/track in 2.5D with the targeted object.

#### 8. Sound Design
- **Audio Track Profile:** The ad is driven by an uninterrupted, high-energy soulful female R&B/pop vocal track over a modern trap/pop beat (syncopated hi-hats, warm sub-bass, and snappy snare claps) where the lyrics literally narrate the medical breakdown (*"You're not fat, you have parasites... They built biofilm walls so your immune system can't touch them..."*).
- **Transition & Sync Hits:** Visual cuts and camera punch-throughs are cut tightly to the **vocal consonants, bass drops, and snare hits** of the song—for example, the golden-eye tunnel plunge in Clip 2 hits on the sustained vocal belt (*"It was never meeee..."*), and the white-gold burst back to the red dress lands squarely on the downbeat of *"The biofilm wall"*. When recreating this style, pair fast zoom transitions with a low sub-bass drop + soft wet/airy whoosh, HUD lock-ons with subtle high-tech crystalline chimes, and biofilm shatters with sparkling glassy fizz/shimmer SFX tucked 6–9 dB under the music track.

## 3. How to Recreate It

### A. The Transitions as Step-by-Step Recipes (at 30 fps)

#### Recipe 1: Room → Body via Floating 3D Cutaway Prop (Clip 1, `0:06.5 – 0:07.5`)
- **Outgoing Clip (Room Shot) Last Frames:**
  - Generate the character scene where a white bubbly sphere floats in front of the characters. Have the camera push in so that during the final 10 frames (`0.33 s`), the camera moves right up to an intact cluster of white bubbles on the lower-left edge of the sphere until the pale peach-white surface fills 95–100% of the frame.
- **Incoming Clip (Inner-World Shot) First Frames:**
  - Start the inner-world clip with the camera positioned inside a cluster of out-of-focus peach-white bubbles filling the foreground for the first 6 frames (`0.20 s`), with the camera pulling backward (or parting the foreground bubbles) to reveal the cylindrical peach tunnel and parasites in the center by frame 10 (`0.33 s`).
- **What to Add in the Edit:**
  - Overlap the two clips by **6 frames (`0.20 s`)** using a smooth **cross-dissolve** while both clips are completely filled with soft-focus cream-peach bubble surfaces (`#FCE5D4`).

### C. Common AI Generation Traps & Exact Wording That Prevents Them

| Trap | What the Model Will Do Wrong | Exact Preventative Wording to Include in Prompts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Floating Diorama / Island in a Void** | Generates a cutaway sphere or tunnel floating as an isolated 3D object against a black, grey, or empty studio background instead of an immersive interior environment. | *"Full-frame immersive interior macro view; the translucent peach tissue walls and out-of-focus foreground bubbles wrap completely around all four edges of the 9:16 frame with zero empty background void visible."* |
| **2. Gory / Visceral Medical Horror** | Renders realistic wet blood, stringy mucus, slimy veins, jagged teeth on parasites, or horror-movie flesh textures. | *"Clean luxury skincare and supplement CGI aesthetic; dry-touch high-gloss silicone, blown pearl glass, soft matte gummy-candy, and liquid honey materials; soft rounded forms with warm peach subsurface scattering; no blood, no veins, no slimy mucus, no horror gore."* |
| **3. Flat 2D Clinical / Textbook Diagram** | Produces flat, unlit educational textbook illustrations, matte plastic shaders, or hard-edged vector infographics. | *"Physically based 3D Octane/Redshift macro render with deep refractive glass shaders (`IOR 1.5`), heavy warm subsurface scattering, strong volumetric backlighting from the center vanishing point, and shallow `f/2.0` macro depth of field with creamy foreground and background bokeh."* |
| **4. Garbled AI HUD Text Baked Into the 3D Mesh** | Image and video models often misspell HUD text (even the original ad has AI-baked typos like `CARVACOL` and `CLEEAR`) or warp thin white circles during camera movement. | **Best Practice:** Generate clean 3D plates using *"no text, no HUD, no labels, no watermarks"* and composite crisp vector HUD overlays in post. **If generating HUD in-model:** *"Flat 2D pure-white (`#FFFFFF`) vector heads-up-display overlay centered in the middle of the frame: a thin 1px white circular targeting reticle and a thin white pill-shaped rounded rectangle outline containing crisp, centered, all-caps geometric sans-serif text reading 'LOCKED'."* |
| **5. Hard Metallic Sci-Fi Spaceship** | Turns the softgel ship in Clip 3 into an opaque grey/silver metal spaceship instead of a translucent amber oil capsule. | *"The craft's body is a smooth, oval, translucent ruby-amber gelatin softgel capsule filled with glowing liquid golden oil and internal light rings—only minimal hairline copper wire accents and two small rear thruster nozzles are metallic."* |
