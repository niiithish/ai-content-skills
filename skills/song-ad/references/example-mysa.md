# Worked example: Mysa sea buckthorn oil (the quality bar, not a template)

**Product:** omega-7 softgels for vaginal dryness, hormone-free (safe with hormone blockers after breast cancer). **Sub-avatar:** Denise, 53, Black American, married 25 years, 14 months after chemo, on hormone blockers. Private fear: "I'm not a woman anymore, and he's going to find one who is." **Mid tier, about 6:00. Soul-gospel, 74 BPM throughout, C minor → E♭ major at the product → F major at the climax.**

What to copy from it:

- **The hook escalates three times in five lines:** left for the best friend, then after chemo, then "'cause I'm the one who told her".
- **The betrayer knows the hero's most private secret.** A second betrayal lands at the midpoint: she had the solution all along and hid it. That makes the product the thing that was stolen.
- **The refrain is built from the insult** ("Some women just dry up"). It returns as belief, then surrender, then flips at the end ("Some women don't dry up").
- **The mentor has a credential** (an oncology nurse for 30 years), and the reframe comes before the product.
- **The doubt line writes itself:** "I am not taking what SHE takes."
- **The climax reverses the insult** in front of the same people: "I didn't dry up, Marcus. I just stopped watering a dead man."
- **Sexual confidence shows without anything explicit:** new sheets, a kiss by the car, "I didn't brace."
- **The music tracks the story:** the drums drop at the mentor, the key lifts to major at the product, then steps up again at the vindication.

### Generation tips
- **Test the voice first.** Generate sections 1–3 only (~74s) and check that the lead vocal stays the same person across section boundaries. Voice consistency across chunks isn't documented, so this is the biggest risk. If the voice drifts, generate longer chunks (the verse and chorus together).
- **Keep the chorus melody identical.** Put "same melody as chorus 1" in the styles for chorus 2 and the final chorus. The recurring melody is what makes it sound like a song and not narration.
- **Spoken lines:** mark them `[Spoken]` in the text. If the model sings them anyway, record them with ElevenLabs TTS in a matching voice and drop them onto the instrumental stem.
- **Generate the whole song in one go, then pick the best take.** Starpop's rule is never to regenerate sections separately, because music models aren't deterministic and the seams show. Roll 4–6 full takes and choose the one where the terrace line ("I just stopped watering a dead man") hits hardest. Use inpainting only as a last resort, for a single word or line.
- **Compact style prompt for Suno** (Starpop: under 25 words, never an artist name): `soul gospel ballad, raw smoky female alto with gospel choir, 74 BPM, heartbreak to triumph, warm vintage live-band production`
- **Length check:** 786 sung words ÷ 2.5 words per second ≈ 5:15 of vocals, plus the instrumental and spoken space ≈ 6:00. Lines are 4–9 words, so each line becomes one shot of 2–3.5 seconds.
- **Suno fallback:** the lyrics are over the 3,000-character limit for a single prompt. Split at the bridge (sections 1–6 and 7–15), use "Extend" for the second half, and put the section styles in the `[ ]` tags.
