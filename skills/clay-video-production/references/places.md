# Clay place sheets

Layout, the 3/4 view, what goes on the sheet and the final check: `video-production/references/sheets/place-sheet.md`. It is written for photoreal; what changes for clay. Which places get one: those with characters in two or more shots (`video-plan` step 7); a place seen once is written in full in its still.

- **Render** the place as a handmade miniature stop-motion set: carved and painted wood, matte clay, painted card and felt, with visible fingerprints and tool marks on the clay parts, patterned wallpaper, quilts, mugs, framed art, plants, copper pans. Describe the materials physically; the word "claymation" alone gives a toy-like or CGI result.
- **Real-world layout:** doors, windows, beds and counters sit where they would in a real room and at the puppets' scale; the handmade look is in the materials, not in melted or bent geometry.
- **Light** warm and golden from the room's practicals (lamps, lanterns, window glow, candles) with soft falloff and rich shadows; never flat, even or cool.
- **Look still.** Once the sheet is approved, the first approved still in the place becomes its look still: later shots there pass the sheet and the look still and ask for the change (a new camera, new poses; see `scene-still.md`).
