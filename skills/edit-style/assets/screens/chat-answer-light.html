What are the generations of treatment for vaginal dryness

There are three commonly discussed generations of treatment for vaginal dryness:

1. First generation: Lubricants
Coat the surface to reduce friction during sex. They wear off in about 10 minutes.

2. Second generation: Vaginal moisturizers
Gels and creams that hold moisture on the surface for a day or two, and often leak from applicators.

3. Third generation: Omega-7 from the inside
Sea buckthorn oil taken daily feeds the tissue where new layers form, so it can rebuild instead of being managed.
